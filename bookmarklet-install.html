Duplicate Contact — Horizon

Drag the button below into your browser's bookmarks bar. If your bookmarks bar isn't visible, show it first (Chrome: Cmd+Shift+B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows).

Duplicate Contact
  1. Drag the blue button above up into your bookmarks bar and drop it there.
  2. Open GHL, go to the Horizon sub-account, and open a contact (or check some boxes on the contacts list).
  3. Click the bookmark. A "Duplicate This Contact" (or "Duplicate Selected") button will appear in the bottom-right corner of the page.
  4. Click that button to actually duplicate the contact(s).

If dragging doesn't work in your browser (some block dragging javascript: links), right-click your bookmarks bar → Add Page, name it "Duplicate Contact", and paste this into the URL field:
